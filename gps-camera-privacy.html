GPS Camera Privacy Policy

Package: com.indigo.gpscamera  ·  Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to GPS Camera: Stamp & Compass and explains how information is accessed, used, and processed when you use the app.

Information and permissions used by GPS Camera

  • Camera: used to capture photos through the app.
  • Precise and approximate location: used to provide location-based features such as GPS coordinates, altitude, weather-related information, and location stamps.
  • Photos and media / storage: used to save photos created with the app and to access images you choose to view, edit, or share through app features.
  • Device sensors: device orientation and motion information may be used for compass, direction, and related camera features.
  • Internet and network access: used for online features and third-party services such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting, and purchase-related services.

How we use information

GPS Camera uses information and device permissions to provide the features you request, including capturing and saving photos, displaying or stamping location-related information, providing compass and direction features, showing weather information where available, and maintaining app functionality.

Photos and location information

Your camera, photos, and location information are used to provide GPS Camera features. You can deny or revoke camera, location, and photo/media permissions through Android settings, although features that depend on those permissions may no longer work.

Indigo Apps Co does not sell your photos, precise location information, or personal information.

Analytics, diagnostics, advertising, and other Google services

GPS Camera uses third-party Google services that may process technical information independently of the photos and location information used for the app's core camera features.

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: may collect app usage and interaction information to help us understand how the app is used and improve it.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: may collect crash reports, application state, device information, and installation identifiers to help diagnose crashes and improve stability.
  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob: may process information such as IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers for advertising, analytics, measurement, and fraud prevention.
  • Google Play Billing: is used to process in-app purchases. Payment information is handled by Google Play; Indigo Apps Co does not receive your full payment-card details.
  • Google Play services: may be used to support location and other Android platform functionality.

Third-party services process information under their own terms and privacy practices. You can learn more at Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Technical information may be transmitted to service providers such as Google when their SDKs are used for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, billing, or related app services. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies and applicable consent or device settings.

Support communications

If you contact us for support, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message content, and any attachments you send. We use this information to respond to your request and provide support.

Data retention

Photos you create are saved according to the storage choices and capabilities of your device. Support communications are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request and maintain appropriate records. Data processed by third-party services is retained according to those providers' policies and settings.

Your choices

  • You can review, allow, or revoke app permissions through Android settings.
  • You can manage advertising and privacy choices through your device and, where presented, consent controls in the app or Google services.
  • You can remove photos saved on your device using your device's normal photo or file-management tools.

Children's privacy

GPS Camera is not directed to children under 13, or under the applicable minimum age of digital consent in a user's country. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children outside the operation of the third-party services described above.

Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the app and rely on established platform and service providers for services such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting, billing, and distribution. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
