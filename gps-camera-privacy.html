GPS Camera Privacy Policy

Package: com.indigo.gpscamera  ·  Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to GPS Camera and explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information and permissions used by GPS Camera

  • Camera: used to capture photos through the app.
  • Location: used when you choose features that display or stamp location-related information such as coordinates and altitude on a photo.
  • Device sensors: orientation and compass-related sensor information may be used to provide direction and compass features.
  • Photos and media / storage: used as needed to save photos created with the app and to access content you choose to use with the app.

How this information is used

  • To provide the camera, location stamp, compass, coordinate, altitude, and related features you request.
  • To save the photos you create to your device.
  • To maintain app stability, troubleshoot issues, and improve performance.

Photos and location information

  • Photos and the information you choose to place on them are handled for the purpose of creating and saving your photo.
  • Indigo Apps Co does not sell your photos, location information, or personal data.
  • Location permission can be denied or revoked at any time in your device settings. Location-dependent features may then be unavailable.

Third-party services

GPS Camera may rely on Google or other platform services for functions such as app distribution, advertising, diagnostics, or performance. Those services may process information such as device identifiers, app activity, diagnostics, or other technical data according to their own privacy policies and your device or consent settings.

Support messages

If you contact us for support, we receive the information you choose to send, including your email address and the contents of your message, so that we can respond.

Your choices

  • You can review, allow, or revoke app permissions through Android settings.
  • You can choose whether to use features that depend on location or access to photos and media.

Data retention

Support communications are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request and maintain appropriate records. Data handled by third-party services is retained according to those services' own policies.

Children's privacy

GPS Camera is not directed to children under 13, or under the applicable minimum age of digital consent in a user's country. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its features, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
