Angle Meter: Inclinometer Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android and iOS versions of Angle Meter: Inclinometer and explains how information is accessed, used, and processed when you use the app.

Information and device features used by Inclinometer

  • Accelerometer and compass sensors: used to provide angle, orientation, and inclinometer functionality.
  • Local storage: the app may store saved measurements, settings, and other app data locally on your device so you can access them later. On older Android versions, storage permission may be used where required by the operating system.
  • Internet access: used for advertising and other third-party services that require a network connection.

Camera and location

Angle Meter: Inclinometer does not request camera permission or precise/approximate location permission for its core inclinometer functionality.

Third-party advertising services may use technical information, such as an IP address, to estimate a device's general location as described below.

How we use information

Device sensor information is used to calculate and display measurements and orientation-related features inside the app.

Saved measurements, settings, and related app data are stored locally on your device for app functionality.

Advertising and Google services

Angle Meter: Inclinometer uses Google Mobile Ads / AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob may automatically process technical information such as IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers for purposes such as advertising, ad measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention.

An IP address may be used to estimate a device's general location.

Depending on your region and applicable privacy requirements, the app may present privacy or consent choices related to advertising. On iOS, the operating system may also present Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission where applicable.

Your advertising experience may vary depending on your consent choices, device settings, region, and applicable law.

Google processes information according to its own terms and privacy practices. For more information, see the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Where paid features or purchases are offered, transactions are processed by the applicable platform provider:

  • Android: Google Play Billing / Google Play
  • iOS: Apple In-App Purchase / App Store

Indigo Apps Co does not receive your full payment-card details. Payment processing is handled by Google or Apple according to their respective terms and privacy policies.

Analytics and crash reporting

The current app configuration does not enable Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics.

Advertising services such as Google Mobile Ads may process diagnostic, interaction, measurement, and other technical information as described in this Privacy Policy and in the service provider's documentation.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Technical information may be processed by third-party service providers when services such as Google Mobile Ads, Google Play Billing, Apple In-App Purchase, or related platform services are used.

Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies, consent requirements, device settings, and applicable law.

Support communications

If you contact us for support, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message content, and any attachments you send.

We use this information to respond to your request and provide support.

Data retention

Saved app data remains on your device until you delete it through the app or your device, where applicable.

Support communications are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request and maintain appropriate records.

Data processed by third-party services is retained according to those providers' policies and settings.

Your privacy choices

  • You can manage app permissions through Android or iOS device settings.
  • You can manage advertising and privacy choices through your device settings and, where available, privacy or consent controls presented by the app or Google services.
  • On iOS, you can manage tracking permission through Apple's privacy settings.
  • Where required, the app may provide access to advertising privacy options so you can review or change applicable consent choices.
  • You can remove locally saved app data using available controls in the app or your device settings, where applicable.

Children's privacy

Angle Meter: Inclinometer is not directed to children under 13, or under the applicable minimum age of digital consent in a user's country.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children outside the operation of the third-party services described above.

Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the app and rely on established platform and service providers for advertising, billing, and distribution.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its features, third-party services, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
