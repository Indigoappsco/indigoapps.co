Angle Meter: Inclinometer Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to Angle Meter: Inclinometer and explains how information is accessed, used, and processed when you use the app.

Information and device features used by Angle Meter: Inclinometer

  • Accelerometer and compass sensors: used to provide angle, orientation, and inclinometer functionality.
  • Local storage: the app may store saved app data on your device so you can access it later. On older Android versions, storage permission may be used where required by the operating system.
  • Internet access: used for Firebase Crashlytics to send crash and diagnostic information when needed to help improve app stability.

Camera and location

Angle Meter: Inclinometer does not request Android camera permission or precise/approximate location permission for its core features.

How we use information

Device sensor information is used to calculate and display measurements and orientation-related features inside the app. Saved app data is stored locally on your device for features such as saved measurements or records.

Crash reporting and diagnostics

Angle Meter: Inclinometer uses Firebase Crashlytics to help us identify crashes, diagnose technical problems, and improve app stability. Crashlytics may automatically process information such as crash logs, diagnostics, app state, device information, app performance information, and installation identifiers.

This information is used for app analytics and diagnostics related to stability and performance. It is processed by Google/Firebase under their own privacy practices. You can learn more at Firebase Privacy and Security.

Advertising and purchases

The current version of Angle Meter: Inclinometer does not display advertising and does not offer in-app purchases.

If a future version changes these practices, we will update this Privacy Policy to reflect the changes.

Analytics

The current version does not use Firebase Analytics. Crashlytics is used only for crash reporting, diagnostics, and app stability as described above.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Technical crash and diagnostic information may be processed by Google/Firebase when Firebase Crashlytics is used. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

Support communications

If you contact us for support, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message content, and any attachments you send. We use this information to respond to your request and provide support.

Data retention

Saved app data remains on your device until you delete it through the app or your device, where applicable. Support communications are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request and maintain appropriate records. Data processed by third-party services is retained according to those providers' policies and settings.

Your choices

  • You can manage app permissions through Android settings.
  • You can remove locally saved app data using the app's available delete controls or your device settings, where applicable.

Age requirement

Angle Meter: Inclinometer is intended for users aged 18 and over.

Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the app and rely on established platform and service providers for crash reporting, diagnostics, and distribution. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
