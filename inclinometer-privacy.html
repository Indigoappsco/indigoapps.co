Angle Meter: Inclinometer Privacy Policy

Package: com.indigo.simpleinclinometer  ·  Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to Angle Meter: Inclinometer and explains how information is accessed, used, and processed when you use the app.

Information and device features used by Inclinometer

  • Accelerometer and compass sensors: used to provide angle, orientation, and inclinometer functionality.
  • Local storage: the app may store saved app data on your device so you can access it later. On older Android versions, storage permission may be used where required by the operating system.
  • Internet access: used for advertising and other third-party services that require a network connection.

Camera and location

Angle Meter: Inclinometer does not request Android camera permission or precise/approximate location permission for its core features.

How we use information

Device sensor information is used to calculate and display measurements and orientation-related features inside the app. Saved app data is stored locally on your device for features such as saved measurements or records.

Advertising and Google services

Angle Meter: Inclinometer uses Google Mobile Ads / AdMob. AdMob may automatically process technical information such as IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers for advertising, analytics, measurement, and fraud prevention. An IP address may be used to estimate a device's general location.

Advertising services process information under their own terms and privacy practices. You can learn more at Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

The app uses Google Play Billing for purchases or paid features where offered. Payment information is processed by Google Play. Indigo Apps Co does not receive your full payment-card details.

Analytics and crash reporting

The current app configuration does not enable Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Technical information may be processed by Google when AdMob, Google Play Billing, or related Google services are used. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies, consent requirements, device settings, and applicable law.

Support communications

If you contact us for support, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message content, and any attachments you send. We use this information to respond to your request and provide support.

Data retention

Saved app data remains on your device until you delete it through the app or your device, where applicable. Support communications are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request and maintain appropriate records. Data processed by third-party services is retained according to those providers' policies and settings.

Your choices

  • You can manage app permissions through Android settings.
  • You can manage advertising and privacy choices through your device and, where presented, consent controls in the app or Google services.
  • You can remove locally saved app data using the app's available delete controls or your device settings, where applicable.

Children's privacy

Angle Meter: Inclinometer is not directed to children under 13, or under the applicable minimum age of digital consent in a user's country. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children outside the operation of the third-party services described above.

Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the app and rely on established platform and service providers for advertising, billing, and distribution. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
