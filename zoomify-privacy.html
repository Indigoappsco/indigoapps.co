Zoomify Privacy Policy

Package: com.indigo.zoomify  ·  Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to Zoomify and explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information and permissions used by Zoomify

  • Camera: used to capture photos and provide the app's camera and zoom features.
  • Photos and media / storage: used as needed to save photos created with the app and to access content you choose to use with the app.

How this information is used

  • To provide camera, zoom, stabilization, enhancement, and related photo features you request.
  • To save photos you create or export through the app.
  • To maintain app stability, troubleshoot issues, and improve performance.

Photos and content

  • Your photos are handled for the purpose of providing the photo and camera features you choose to use.
  • Indigo Apps Co does not sell your photos or personal data.
  • You can revoke camera or photo/media permissions at any time through your device settings. Features that depend on those permissions may then be unavailable.

Third-party services

Zoomify may rely on Google or other platform services for functions such as app distribution, advertising, diagnostics, or performance. Those services may process information such as device identifiers, app activity, diagnostics, or other technical data according to their own privacy policies and your device or consent settings.

Support messages

If you contact us for support, we receive the information you choose to send, including your email address and the contents of your message, so that we can respond.

Your choices

  • You can review, allow, or revoke app permissions through Android settings.
  • You can choose whether to allow camera and photo/media access.

Data retention

Support communications are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request and maintain appropriate records. Data handled by third-party services is retained according to those services' own policies.

Children's privacy

Zoomify is not directed to children under 13, or under the applicable minimum age of digital consent in a user's country. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its features, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
